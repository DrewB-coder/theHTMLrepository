Generation Three

Gardevoir

Gardevoir is the Embrace Pokemon. It is a Psychic/fairy type pokemon. It is one of two final members of its evolutionary line. The other being a pokemon named Gallade. The first member is Ralts which evolves into Kirlia. It then evolves into Gardevoir at level 30 or, if its male, Gallade when exposed to a Dawn Stone.

Sableye

Sable is the Darkness Pokemon. It is a Dark/Ghost type pokemon. Sable is the only member of its evolutionary line. It can preform Mega Evolution when used with a Sablenite in battle. When this occurs it becomes Mega Sable for the duration of the battle. From its introduction up until generation 6, it didn't have any type weaknesses similar to Spiritomb which has the same typing.
