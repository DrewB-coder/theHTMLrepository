Generation Four

Lucario

Lucario is the Aura Pokemon. It is a Fighting/Steel type pokemon. It is the final member of its evolutionary line. It evolves from Riolu after leveling with a high enough friendship score. As mentioned this is a tedious evolution method. It is one of very few pokemon to possess to different Mega Evolutions. The second evolution was released in the latest pokemon game, Pokemon Legends: Z-A.

Arceus

Arceus is the Alpha pokemon. It is a Normal type pokemon. It is a Mythical Pokemon and does not evolve. It possess a form for each of the 18 different pokemon types. It can switch between these forms while hold a Plate or Z-crystal of the corresponding type. Arcues is the creator of the Pokemon world.
