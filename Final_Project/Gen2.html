Generation Two

Tyranitar

Tyranitar is the Armour Pokemon. It is a Rock/Dark type pokemon. It is the last member of its evolutionary line. The first form being Larvitar which evolves into Pupitar then into Tyranitar. This pokemon is what fans would refer to as a pseudo-legendary pokemon. It is one of only two members of this group that are not at least part Dragon type. The other pokemon being Metagross.

Togepi

Togepi is the Spike Ball Pokemon. It is a Fairy type pokemon. It is the first member of its evolutionary line. It evolves to Togetic when leveled up with a high friendship vaule. Which is considered one of the worst evolution methods due to it being rather tedious. It ten evolves into Togekiss after exposure to a Shiny Stone
