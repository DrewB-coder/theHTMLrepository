Generation One

Gengar

Gengar is the Shadow Pokemon. It is a Ghost/Poison type pokemon. It is the last stage of its evolutionary line. The first stage is Gastly which evolves into Haunter. Haunter then must be traded in order for it to evolve into Gengar. Unless you are in a game where the Link Cable exist.

Pikachu

Pikachu is the Mouse Pokemon. It is a Electric type pokemon. It was the first pokemon Ash aquired on his journey. It was given to him by Professor Oak in the first episode of the pokemon animated series. Pikachu evolves from Pichu and Into Raichu when exposed to a Thunder Stone. It is also the mascot of Pokemon.
